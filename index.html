Our Story

From Hamida's Kitchen, With Love

Hamida's Kitchen began the way all the best food does — with neighbours asking for seconds. What started as cooking for family and friends in Fraser Rise grew into a beloved home kitchen serving Melbourne's west.

Everything is made in small batches with the same care Hamida puts into her own family's dinner: masalas ground fresh, dosa batter fermented overnight, biryanis sealed on dum until the whole street can smell them.

And one promise holds above all others — 100% halal, always. Because when you order from a home kitchen, trust is the most important ingredient.
